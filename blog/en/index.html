ReplayRCA: Measuring Post-hoc Diagnosis and Live Oversight of Agent Failures by Counterfactual Replay

When an agent fails a multi-step task, can another model find the decisive mistake and fix it with a single action? ReplayRCA scores the diagnosis by intervention rather than against annotations: the environment is restored before the chosen step, the proposed action replaces the agent's, the same agent continues, and its success rate is compared with that of a placebo replaying the original action. One of the best models at post-hoc diagnosis was the weakest at live oversight.

Toward Alignment Scaling Laws: A Framework and First Preregistered Measurements

Does alignment get easier or harder as models grow? Treating it as one property hides the answer. Measured per risk, the burden of holding a safety target follows its own scaling exponent, and the fastest-growing corrected risk sets the long-run regime. Two preregistered measurements, on Pythia and on Qwen2.5 from 0.5B to 72B.

DGF-Bench: A Benchmark for Simulating and Auditing Deception Against Multi-Agent Governance Boards

A board of agents reviews synthetic dossiers under 61 executable rules while an attacker plants deceptive content in the evidence the organization does not vouch for. Attacks that imitate the organization's own process passed against four of six models; direct orders and false authority almost never did.
